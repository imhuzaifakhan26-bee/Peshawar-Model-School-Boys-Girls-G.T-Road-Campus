Rules & Regulations

Important rules and regulations for students and parents

1. General Discipline

  • Students must follow the instructions of teachers, school administration and authorized staff.
  • Students are expected to maintain respectful, responsible and disciplined behaviour.
  • Fighting, bullying, threatening behaviour, harassment or deliberately disturbing other students is not permitted.
  • Students must respect school property, classrooms, laboratories, furniture, books and other facilities.

2. Mobile Phones & Electronic Gadgets

  • Mobile phones are not allowed for students during school hours unless specifically authorized by the school administration.
  • Unauthorized smart watches, tablets, gaming devices, cameras, recording devices or other electronic gadgets are also not permitted.
  • Students must not make unauthorized photographs, audio recordings or videos inside the school.
  • If a prohibited device is brought to school, the administration may take disciplinary action according to school policy.

3. Weapons & Dangerous Items

  • Weapons and dangerous objects are strictly prohibited on school premises.
  • Students must not bring knives, firearms, explosive items or any other object intended to cause harm.
  • Any serious safety concern should immediately be reported to a teacher or school administration.
  • Possession of a dangerous or prohibited item may result in serious disciplinary action according to applicable school rules and safety requirements.

4. Academic Integrity

  • Students must complete their own academic work.
  • Cheating, copying, impersonation or using unauthorized materials during examinations is not allowed.
  • Students should bring the required books, notebooks and learning materials to class.
  • Repeated academic misconduct may result in disciplinary action according to school policy.

5. Attendance & Punctuality

  • Students should arrive at school on time.
  • Regular attendance is important for academic progress.
  • Parents/guardians should inform the school about legitimate absences according to school procedures.

6. School Property

  • School furniture, computers, laboratory equipment, library books and other property must be handled carefully.
  • Intentional damage to school property may lead to disciplinary or recovery action according to school policy.

7. Disciplinary Action

The school administration may take appropriate disciplinary action when a student violates school rules or creates a serious safety, academic or disciplinary problem. Depending on the nature and severity of the matter, action may include counselling, warning, parent/guardian notification, confiscation of prohibited items, suspension or other measures permitted under the school's applicable policies. Serious or repeated violations may affect a student's continuation at the school. Any specific long-term restriction on study or admission would be determined by the competent school administration under its applicable rules and should not be assumed from this website alone.

8. Student Safety

Students should immediately inform a teacher or authorized staff member if they notice a dangerous situation, bullying, harassment, prohibited item or other serious concern. The purpose of these rules is to maintain a safe, respectful and productive learning environment for all students.

School Facilities

Educational, academic, transport, sports and student-support facilities

School Transport

The School has its own pick and drop facility for students. An incharge in each bus looks after the children during their journey to and from home and school. Current transport charges can be enquired from the school office. Transport fee is payable for 10 months in a year.

School Tuckshop

The School Tuck Shop provides hygienically prepared eatables to students at reasonable charges. Parents of Kindercare, Nursery and Prep students are advised to provide healthy or homemade food to their children instead of pocket money.

Library

The school library provides students with a calm and quiet environment in which they can supplement their knowledge through reading. A collection of books is available for students and teachers. Newspapers in English and Urdu, periodicals and magazines can also be available for reading and educational enrichment.

School Dispensary

The school provides a dispensary facility to support students' basic medical needs. Qualified medical staff may assist with children's health requirements and maintain relevant health records. Periodic medical check-ups may also be conducted according to the school's arrangements.

Video Library

The video library facility is designed to provide educational and entertaining video content for young students. Visual learning can help children understand stories, concepts and general educational topics in a friendly learning environment.

Computer Laboratory

Computer laboratories provide students with opportunities to develop computer knowledge and practical digital skills. Qualified instructors can guide students in learning computer fundamentals and other technology skills according to educational requirements.

Science Laboratories

Science laboratory facilities support practical learning in subjects such as Biology, Chemistry and Physics. Students can perform relevant educational activities under the supervision and guidance of qualified science teachers.

Physical Education & Sports

Physical education and sports are an important part of student development. Participation in games and sports supports physical fitness, teamwork, discipline and healthy competition. Different games and sporting activities may be provided according to the school's facilities and programme.

Academics

The school focuses on regular academic work and assessment to support students' educational progress. Academic activities may include classroom work, surprise tests, weekly tests, monthly tests and term examinations. Co-curricular activities can also support interaction between teachers and students.

IGCSE / GCE (O-Level)

The school provides information and educational pathways for students interested in international examination programmes. IGCSE/GCE O-Level is an international qualification route with subjects such as English, Urdu, Mathematics, Biology, Physics, Chemistry, Islamiat and Pakistan Studies, depending on the programme and applicable arrangements.
